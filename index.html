Student Accommodation Rules

This portal helps TVET MARA Besut students choose hostel rooms by block and book one bed quickly.

6 Beds Per Room

All rooms in Block A, B and C have 6 beds in total.

1 Bed Per Student

Each student is allowed to book only 1 bed to ensure fair allocation.

No Price Charged

No payment or nightly price is shown in this system.
